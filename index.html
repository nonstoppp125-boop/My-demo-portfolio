05 / HOW I THINK

Start with a question. Finish with evidence.

A repeatable framework for turning an ambiguous business problem into a transparent analytical investigation.

CASE / 01

Why might sales be changing?

Define the metric, confirm the time period, check data quality, segment results, and investigate plausible drivers before suggesting an explanation.

KPI definitionSegmentationRoot cause
↗
CASE / 02

Where might a funnel lose users?

Define funnel stages, calculate conversion rates with clear denominators, compare stage-to-stage drop-offs, and investigate potential friction points.

ConversionFunnel analysisMetrics
